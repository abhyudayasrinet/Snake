Snake Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Snake is an arcade game provided by GGWP. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Information the game stores

Snake stores game settings and high scores locally on your device. We do not operate an account system and do not require you to provide your name, email address, or other contact details to play.

Advertising

Snake uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to show advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and other information needed to provide, measure, and improve advertising. This processing is governed by Google's policies.

Learn more at Google Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information handled by the advertising SDK may be shared with Google and advertising partners for ad delivery, fraud prevention, measurement, and related purposes.

Children

Snake is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its advertising configuration changes. The current version will always be available at this page.
